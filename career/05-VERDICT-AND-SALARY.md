# Recruiter verdict, and what the UAE salary evidence actually supports

## A. Media Buyer / Paid Social / Performance Marketing Executive

### Verdict: **BORDERLINE — leaning yes for junior roles, no for the 2–4 year postings**

Assessed against what the UAE listings I retrieved actually ask for: hands-on Meta Ads
Manager execution, campaign setup/optimisation/reporting, reading platform metrics,
working with creative, **2–4 years' experience**.

**Why borderline rather than no.** You meet the functional requirements. You have run
Meta campaigns yourself, you can read the metrics, and you have something most junior
applicants lack entirely — you have owned the commercial consequence of your buying, not
just the platform output. The COD margin work is a genuine differentiator, and the ability
to produce your own creative makes you cheaper and faster to deploy on a small team.
Arabic plus English is a real advantage for GCC targeting.

**Why not a straight yes.** Two things, and only two:
1. **Budget scale.** Your shown spend is ~$1,213 total. The 2–4 year postings assume
   someone who has been trusted with a monthly budget. You have no answer to "what's the
   largest monthly spend you've managed?" and that question gets asked every time.
2. **Tracking.** No GA4, GTM or Pixel/CAPI evidence. At the 2-year mark this is assumed.

**Why it would currently be a no on screening, regardless of the above.** The certifications
you have not earned are listed as earned, and the profile implies ~4 years against ~18
months of described work. Those are not fit problems, they are integrity flags, and a
recruiter who spots either stops reading. Fix them and this verdict moves up immediately.

**Suitable seniority:** Junior / Executive. *Media Buyer (Junior)*, *Performance Marketing
Executive*, *Paid Social Executive*, *Digital Marketing Executive (Paid)*, *Media Buying
Assistant*, *Growth Executive*.

**The single most important evidence that would change this to yes:**
A one-page case study on one campaign showing **a before state, one specific change you
made, the after state, and the reasoning** — with the Purchase event correctly defined as
a COD order submission. That single document answers the budget objection by
demonstrating judgement instead of scale, and it proves you understand attribution better
than most candidates with twice your tenure. It is worth more than any certificate you
could earn this month.

**Second most important:** a UAE mobile number, confirmed visa status, and one monthly
budget figure you can honestly name — even AED 3,000/month for one small local client.

## B. Creative Strategist

### Verdict: **NOT YET**

Assessed against the UAE listings: 2–3 years social content management, concept ideation
for photo and video, scripting, content briefs with mood boards and references, short-form
video for TikTok/Reels/Shorts, strong 3-second hooks, native UGC pacing.

**Why not yet.** You can produce, and the 61.08% hook rate shows you watch the right
metric. But creative strategy is hired on evidence that creative *decisions* were made and
tested — a documented angle, a hypothesis, variants, a winner, and the next round it
produced. Every creative asset you have shown me is a finished visual output: a concept
(Parker's), an unconfirmed project (COBA), and a personal showreel. None demonstrates a
tested decision. That is the whole job, and it is the thing missing.

Two secondary problems. You have no creative briefs or storyboards as artefacts, so there
is nothing to show for the part of the role that is documentation. And the UAE market for
this title skews toward social content management with a stated preference for real
estate, luxury hospitality and high-end agency backgrounds — none of which you have.

**Why "not yet" rather than "no".** The gap is documentation, not capability. You have
almost certainly already made the decisions; you just have not written them down. Two
artefacts would move this to borderline within a month, which is unusually fast.

**Suitable seniority:** Junior. *Creative Strategist (Junior)*, *UGC / Creative
Coordinator*, *Content & Creative Executive*, *Performance Creative Associate*. Target
UGC-led e-commerce brands and performance agencies with in-house creative pods — not
luxury or real-estate social agencies, whose stated background preference you do not match.

**The single most important evidence that would change this:**
One creative test breakdown — the same campaign as the 61.08% hook rate. Show the hook you
chose, the two or three you tested it against, why you believed it would win, what the
result was, and what you tested next. Add one written creative brief and one
storyboard-to-final-cut comparison. Three documents, maybe two weekends.

### Caveats that matter

- **Only one of these is an advertised range.** The UGC Creative Coordinator posting at
  AED 6,000–6,500 is the sole figure attached to a real job. Everything else is an
  aggregator estimate built from self-reported data, which in the UAE skews small and
  unreliable.
- **The Glassdoor figure was labelled "per year."** AED 14,563/year is roughly AED
  1,200/month, which is not a real salary for this role in Dubai — it is a units error on
  their side. I am reading it as monthly because that is the only coherent interpretation,
  but you should treat it as soft.
- **Benefits are usually excluded and vary enormously.** In the UAE, whether the package
  includes housing allowance, medical insurance, annual flights and visa sponsorship can
  swing the real value by 20–40%. Always ask what is basic and what is total.
- **I cannot infer your salary from your profile.** Anyone who gives you a precise number
  from a CV is guessing.

### What I will actually say

For a **junior media buyer / performance marketing executive** in Dubai with your evidence
base — roughly 18 months, no budget-scale record, no certifications yet — the advertised
junior end of this market sits around **AED 6,000–10,000 per month basic**, and the
AED 8,000–15,000 "specialist" band is where you would be aiming after 12–18 months in a
UAE role with a real budget behind you. For the **creative strategist / UGC coordinator**
track, the one live posting I found was AED 6,000–6,500.

That is a directional read from thin evidence, not a benchmark. Before you negotiate
anything, get better data than this: check 10–15 live postings for your exact target
titles on Bayt and LinkedIn and record the ranges where they are published, and ask two
recruiters directly. That is an hour of work and it will beat every number in this table.
