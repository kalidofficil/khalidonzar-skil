### Use Option 1.

Why, specifically:

- **It front-loads two searchable job titles.** UAE recruiters filter on "Media Buyer" and "Performance Marketing Executive". "Full-Funnel Growth" in your current headline is not a search term anyone uses.
- **"Executive" sets seniority honestly and keeps you in the filter.** Recruiters screening a junior req discard profiles that read senior; recruiters screening senior reqs were never going to shortlist you. Naming the level puts you in the right pile rather than the wrong one.
- **It drops Google Ads.** Your current headline promises Meta *and* Google equally. You cannot defend the Google half, and the first interview question will find that out. Removing it costs you almost nothing in search and removes a live risk.
- **It adds e-commerce**, which is the actual shape of all your experience and a strong filter term for D2C and COD employers.
- **The last clause is the differentiator.** "I build my own creative" is concrete, rare at this level, verifiable from your Featured section, and it is what makes a hiring manager read the rest. Option 3 promises a second discipline at equal weight, which at 18 months invites scrutiny you do not need.

Also: turn on **Open to Work**, set to *Media Buyer, Performance Marketing Executive, Paid Social Specialist, Digital Marketing Executive*, location Dubai/UAE, recruiters-only if you prefer discretion.
