## Top Skills — pin these three

1. **Meta Ads** 2. **Performance Marketing** 3. **E-commerce**

Replacing the current TikTok Marketing / Google Ads / Performance Marketing. Your two
weakest channels are currently the first things a recruiter sees.

## Skill gaps, in the order worth closing

1. **GA4 + GTM + Pixel/CAPI.** The clearest gap between you and a 2-year media buyer, and
   the fastest to close. Google's free GA4 certification plus Meta Blueprint's tracking
   modules. Then document fixing one real event on your own site — that document is worth
   more to you than the certificate.
2. **A managed monthly budget.** The gap you cannot study your way out of. The only routes
   are: freelance/volunteer for one small UAE business, or get hired junior and grow into
   it. Treat it as the reason to take a junior title rather than hold out.
3. **Google Ads Search.** You are already advertising it. Either earn it or stop claiming it.
4. **Snapchat Ads.** Materially bigger in GCC than in most markets, and few applicants have it.
5. **Creative briefs and storyboards as artefacts.** Two documents would unlock the entire
   creative-strategist track.
6. **UAE/GCC market fluency.** AOV, payment mix, Ramadan and seasonal cycles, Arabic vs
   English creative performance. Cheap to acquire, and it shows in interview.

Do not add a skill to your profile because a JD asks for it. Every listed skill is a
question you have invited.
