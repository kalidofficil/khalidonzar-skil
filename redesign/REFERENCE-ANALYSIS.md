# Reference analysis: the three cinematic website videos

Frame-by-frame study of Khalid's three design references, done by extracting
frames at 0.5 second intervals across each clip and at 0.15 second intervals
across two key transitions. Timestamps below are from those extractions.

These are Khalid's design references. None of them is his own work.

A note that governs everything here: these are **recordings of screens**, filmed
on a physical camera. What can be observed is what the pixels do. What cannot be
observed is how any of it was built. Nothing below claims to know the technology.

---

## 1. `3e2c312e-video1.mp4` — juice brand. Opened. 9.89s, 720x1280, 30fps.

**Opening scene.** A laptop on a desk in a dark room. Behind it, heavily blurred
neon signage, including a script "Juice" logo in orange and blocks of cyan,
magenta and green light. The screen is the brightest thing in frame.

**Subject.** A bottle of orange juice, centred, standing in a crown of splashing
juice.

**Typography.** Heavy condensed uppercase grotesque. "TASTE THE FUTURE." set very
large, white with "FUTURE." in amber. Small sans for the nav. Later pages use a
lighter, sentence-case sans.

**Lighting and colour.** Near-black page ground, product lit like a studio
still-life with warm rim light. Amber, orange, deep charcoal. The lower content
page inverts to a bright saturated orange with white cards.

**Observed movement, with timestamps.**
- 00:00–00:02 The splash crown grows and resolves around the bottle. The headline
  is **behind the bottle**: at 00:00 the bottle occludes the middle of "TASTE TH…";
  by 00:01 the full lockup reads with the bottle still in front of the letters.
- 00:02.5 A hand enters the frame and rests on the keyboard.
- 00:02.5–00:03 The page changes to a two-column layout, headline left, body right,
  bottle centred and smaller.
- 00:03.5 The bottle appears alone and upright, contents visible.
- 00:04–00:05.5 Fruit bursts outward from the bottle: kiwi, strawberry, orange
  slices, blueberries, with liquid and ice.
- 00:05.4–00:06.9 The dark hero crossfades into a bright orange product page.
  At roughly 00:06.2 both grounds are visible at once, mid-blend. The physical
  camera pushes toward the screen through this stretch, then pulls back.
- 00:07.5–00:09.5 The orange page with product cards holds while the camera
  settles back to a three-quarter view.

**Inferred, not observed.** That the burst and the crossfade are tied to scrolling
at all. No scrollbar, cursor or wheel motion is visible. The hand rests on the
keyboard rather than visibly scrolling. This could equally be an autoplaying video
inside the page, a timeline animation, or a rendered mockup.

## 2. `4dad746c-video_2.mp4` — "Dolce Nero". Opened. 9.52s, 720x1280, 24fps.

**Opening scene.** A laptop on a wooden café table, cookies scattered beside it.
The background is a warm café interior thrown far out of focus.

**Subject.** A single scoop of white ice cream on a plate, centred.

**Typography.** A high-contrast display serif. "DOLCE NERO" upper left in caps
with generous letterspacing, a small italic kicker above it. "Midnight Sundae"
upper right in the same serif, title case. Small sans for nav, one cream pill
button top right. The most restrained and most editorial of the three.

**Lighting and colour.** Deep chocolate and burgundy ground, warm low-key lighting,
caramel and cream highlights. No cool tones anywhere.

**Observed movement, with timestamps.**
- 00:00 A stream of dark chocolate begins pouring from the top of the frame.
- 00:00–00:02.5 The stream descends and begins coating the scoop.
- 00:02.5–00:04.5 The coating completes. The scoop is now a smooth glossy dark
  dome sitting in a pool of chocolate.
- 00:04.5–00:05.1 **The dome cracks open.** Sampled at 0.15s: 04.50 smooth,
  04.65 first fracture at the top, 04.80 the crack widens, 04.95 the shell
  splits into shards, 05.10 the interior is revealed.
- 00:04.95–00:05.4 **The typography re-composes during the crack.** "DOLCE NERO"
  on the left fades out, and "Midnight Sundae" travels from the right side into
  the left headline position. Two type blocks become one.
- 00:05.4–00:09 The revealed interior, brownie and whipped cream, with caramel
  pouring over it. The framing tightens slightly.

**Inferred, not observed.** Everything about the mechanism. The whole clip is one
continuous transformation with no cuts, which is consistent with a scrubbed video,
but the recording cannot tell anyone that. The physical camera barely moves here,
which is what makes this the cleanest read of the three.

## 3. `1120d74b-video_3.mp4` — "Tikka Masala" jar. Opened. 9.38s, 720x1280, 30fps.

**Opening scene.** A laptop shot from a low angle, screen filling most of the
frame. Behind it, a wall lit in a green-to-blue aurora gradient.

**Subject.** A dark jar of tikka masala sauce.

**Typography.** Heavy condensed uppercase, frequently outlined, in bright orange
and yellow on deep red. Poster-loud. Later sections switch to light cream pages
with quieter headings.

**Lighting and colour.** Deep crimson ground, bright orange, hot magenta, cream.
The most saturated and highest-energy of the three.

**Observed movement, with timestamps.**
- 00:00 The jar sits centre-left, upright.
- 00:00.5 "A NEW ANGLE OF FLAVOUR" appears while the jar tilts.
- 00:01 The jar has rotated to a top-down view showing the lid.
- 00:01.5–00:02 Headline switches to "BOLD FLAVOR / TIKKA MASALA" and the jar
  returns upright.
- 00:02.5–00:04.5 The cycle repeats through further orientations: tilted, upright,
  lid-down, tilted again, with the headline changing to "UNWRAP THE ADVENTURE" at
  00:03.5. **The jar is seen from many distinct angles across the clip.**
- 00:05 The jar tips as if pouring, under magenta light.
- 00:05.5 A real photographed hand holds the jar, over "JUST BOLD TASTE".
- 00:06–00:07 The page turns light and cream with food photography cards, then
  "WHY THE JAR MATTERS" over text columns.
- 00:07.5–00:09 Magenta panels, several jars together, "UNLOCK THE MAGIC INSIDE
  THE JAR", then "LET'S GET COOKING", then a light recipe-card page.

**Inferred, not observed.** That the rotation is interactive or scroll-linked.
It could be a rendered turntable, a sequence of stills, or real-time. The
recording shows angles changing in sync with headline changes; it does not show
what drives them.

## What the three genuinely share

1. **One hero object, centred, treated with reverence.** Never a scene, never a
   montage. One thing, big, lit like a studio still-life.
2. **The background is a flat colour field.** Chocolate, crimson, near-black.
   Not one of the three uses atmosphere, haze, weather or an environment behind
   the subject. The drama is entirely in the object and the light on it.
3. **The object changes state continuously while the type holds or re-composes.**
   Coating, cracking, bursting, rotating.
4. **Type and object share depth.** The juice headline sits behind the bottle.
5. **Dark, saturated hero, then a bright readable content page.** All three make
   this turn, and all three stop performing once they get there.
6. **A real human hand appears** in two of the three, as a warmth device.

## The problem this creates, stated plainly

All three references are **product sites**. A bottle, a dessert, a jar. Products
can rotate, melt, crack open and explode. **A person cannot**, and Khalid's brief
rules out exactly that. So the literal treatments do not transfer. Copying them
onto a human face would produce either the face morphing he forbade, or a gimmick.

What transfers is the grammar, not the trick: one subject held centre, a flat
saturated ground, type sharing its depth, a continuous state change carried by
something that is allowed to change, and the turn into a bright readable page.

**For a person, the thing allowed to change is the light.**

## Reference → observed → adaptation → implementation

| Reference | Observed effect | Proposed adaptation | Implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Juice, 00:00–00:02 | Headline sits behind the bottle; the product occludes the letters | "KHALID" oversized behind the portrait; head and shoulder cut through it | Layered photography. The cutout PNG on a higher z-index than the type. Already made and verified. |
| Dolce Nero, 00:00–00:05.1 | Subject transforms continuously, scoop to dome to cracked-open | Light sweeps across the portrait, edge-light to full key, as one continuous scroll-linked change | Real-time code. A warm gradient masked to the cutout's alpha, its position driven by scroll, brightness riding alongside. No pixel of the face is altered. |
| Dolce Nero, 00:04.95–00:05.4 | Two type blocks re-compose into one as the subject changes | Name and positioning line re-compose as the light lands | Real-time code. Scroll-driven transforms and opacity on split spans. |
| Jar, throughout | Subject seen from many angles, synced to changing headlines | **Not adopted from one photograph.** Needs 3 to 5 real photographs to do honestly | Would be layered photography with cross-dissolves between real shots. Flagged as a request, not assumed. |
| Jar + Juice | Deep saturated flat colour ground | A deep ember ground, warm, flat, no atmosphere | CSS. Nothing generated. |
| All three | Dark hero turns into a bright readable content page | The ground climbs from near-black through clay to bone as the visitor descends | CSS, scroll-linked. |
| Juice, 00:04–00:05.5 | Elements burst outward in depth | Campaign figures arrive at three parallax depths, farthest slightly defocused | Real-time code. Transform and blur on layered cards. |
| Juice 00:02.5, Jar 00:05.5 | A real human hand for warmth | Khalid himself is the human element, so this is already satisfied | Layered photography. |

## Honest labelling of every technique proposed

- **Layered photographic depth:** the portrait cutout, its occlusion with type,
  its parallax plane, the framed still. This is 2D compositing. It is not 3D.
- **Real-time code:** the light sweep, the type choreography, the ground's colour
  climb, the evidence cards, the section rule. Plain CSS and JavaScript.
- **Interactive 3D:** none proposed. Nothing in the references justifies a mesh
  for a human subject, and a generated 3D head would break the likeness rule.
- **Generated video:** **none needed for this direction.** The references' hero
  grounds are flat colour fields, so there is nothing to generate behind him.
  This is a reversal of the earlier plan and it comes from the reference study,
  not from convenience.

## Where this leaves the studio concept

The studio-with-atmosphere idea proposed earlier does **not** match the
references, and should be dropped. Two reasons, both from the frames above:

1. Not one of the three references puts atmosphere behind the subject. Their
   grounds are flat. A hazy studio is the same mistake as the storm, one step
   quieter: still a moving background with a photograph in front of it.
2. All three are considerably bolder in colour than the restrained night-and-gold
   palette proposed earlier. Following the references means committing to a
   saturated ground, not a tasteful dark grey one.
