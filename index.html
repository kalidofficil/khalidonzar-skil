Process

How a partnership runs.

Four stages, in order. The fourth is the one most buyers skip, and the one that makes me safe to bring into a client relationship you own.

  1. 01

    Handover

    NDA and white-label terms first. Then I read the account, the tracking and the client's commercials, and tell you plainly what I see — including when the problem isn't the media buying.

  2. 02

    Triage

    The first two weeks: what's broken, what's putting the account at risk, and the three changes worth making before your next client call. Written so you can present it as your own work.

  3. 03

    Run

    I buy the media, you own the relationship. Budget moves against marginal return and blended MER. Weekly notes written for your account manager to forward, not for me to look busy.

  4. 04

    Handback

    Structure, naming conventions, dashboards and playbooks documented, so your team can take the account back whenever you want it. No lock-in, no hostage situation.

About

Your client should never need to know I exist.

I've spent seven years buying media — agency side first, then in-house at a DTC brand where I owned the acquisition P&L. That in-house year changed how I work: when you sit next to the finance team, you stop reporting platform ROAS and start defending contribution margin.

Since 2023 I've worked white-label only. In practice that means I sign your NDA before I see an account, I don't take direct clients who compete with a partner, and my name isn't on the dashboard, the deck or anything the client receives — yours is. I'd rather hand an account back documented than keep it dependent on me.
