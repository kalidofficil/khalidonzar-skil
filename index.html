What the numbers are

A reported purchase is not a delivered order.

What a “purchase” is, and is not. A reported purchase in Ads Manager is not automatically a delivered or profitable cash-on-delivery order. On COD a share of reported orders are never confirmed, never delivered, or returned at the door.

So this page shows no revenue, no profit, no ROAS, no delivery rate and no confirmation rate — I do not hold verified data for those, and inventing them would make everything else here worthless. Client names, account names, campaign names and business identifiers are withheld.

The reading

The creative worked; the promise did not carry. A 61.08% hook rate says the first frames earned attention at scale. A 2.29% link CTR against that says the ad never converted attention into intent.

What I changed

Shorten the distance between the hook and the offer, put price and guarantee in the creative rather than on the page, and tighten placements even at a higher CPM. Case A and case B are that discipline applied.

Campaign concept

Hair salon

This has not run. It shows how I think about a brief before any money is spent — not a result. A local service business with a fixed chair count: the constraint is not reach, it is calendar capacity. An ad that fills Saturday and leaves Tuesday empty has not worked.

Approach

  • Radius tight enough that travel time never becomes the objection
  • Offer built on a first-visit service, not a discount on everything
  • Creative shot in the chair — the result, not the premises

How it would be judged

  • Cost per booking against the average first-visit value
  • Rebooking rate at eight weeks — a salon lives on the second visit
  • Weekday fill rate as its own line, never blended into the total
Sample strategy

Real estate

Also unrun. Long consideration, high value, and a lead a human has to call. The question is not how to get leads cheaply — it is how to get leads an agent will still return calls to in week three.

Approach

  • Browsing intent and viewing intent split, never one blended cost per lead
  • Qualification inside the form, accepting a higher cost per lead on purpose
  • Creative leading with the specific unit, price band and handover date

How it would be judged

  • Cost per qualified lead, defined with the agent before launch
  • Contact rate and viewing rate, reported back from the agent's CRM
  • Cost per viewing as the real number; cost per lead is diagnostic only
